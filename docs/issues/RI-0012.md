### Summary
Tenancy choke, role resolution, membership-scoped sessions. Our adapter authenticates sessions but resolves workspace membership with the server-side owner key for principalId-mode sessions, so an invited teammate would pass the membership check for every workspace; roles are display-only. This slice makes Octo's roles load-bearing at one choke point.

## PDD
The switcher lists only workspaces the reader is an active member of, with role badges. A foreign workspace request is a clear 403 and a join screen, never a filtered-but-200 leak. Actions beyond the reader's role are hidden or disabled with a one-line reason.

## SDD

No new identity store.
