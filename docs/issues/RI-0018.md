### Summary
Single-Octo Slack-like tenancy hierarchy: the entire application runs inside **one** Octo workspace (`OCTO_WORKSPACE_ID`), subdivided in `ri_console` into Organizations, App Workspaces, Projects, and global Users linked via many-to-many memberships. Supersedes the Octo-workspace-per-tenant assumption in RI-0013 (#38) and eliminates the `OCTO_ACCOUNT_KEY` blocker for workspace creation and teammate invites.

## PDD
A user signs in once with their Google identity and belongs to multiple App Workspaces (automatically receiving a private **Personal** workspace plus any shared workspaces such as *Design Bakery* or *GISMO NYC*) and multiple Projects (open to all workspace members or private to invited collaborators, like Slack channels and single-channel guests). Clicking **New workspace…** or **Invite teammate…** works immediately without needing an Octo account key. Contacts, companies, approvals, agents, and files stay scoped per App Workspace so personal or client relationships never bleed across workspaces.

## SDD
1. **Single Octo Container:** All SQL (`ri_console.*`), graph, and file calls target the single deployment `OCTO_WORKSPACE_ID`. App Workspaces are rows in `ri_console`, never separate Octo control-plane workspaces.
2. **Schema additions in `ri_console`:**
   - `ri_console.app_user (id, principal_id, email, display_name, created_at)` — one global row per signed-in human.
   - `ri_console.organization (id, name, slug, created_at)` — top-level grouping kept in DB (hidden in UI switcher until needed).
   - `ri_console.workspace (id, organization_id, name, kind ['personal' | 'team'], created_by, created_at)` — App Workspaces shown in the top-left switcher.
   - `ri_console.organization_member (organization_id, user_id, role ['owner' | 'admin' | 'member'])`
   - `ri_console.workspace_member (workspace_id, user_id, email, role ['owner' | 'admin' | 'operator' | 'member'], status ['active' | 'pending'], invited_by, created_at)`
   - `ri_console.project_member (project_id, user_id, role ['lead' | 'editor' | 'viewer'])` — optional override for `visibility = 'private'` projects and cross-workspace collaborators.
3. **Tenancy Choke (`app/server/tenancy.ts`):** `resolveTenancy` resolves the caller's global `app_user`, loads active `workspace_member` rows from `ri_console`, auto-provisions their private `Personal` workspace on first sign-in, and enforces `403 WORKSPACE_NOT_A_MEMBER` on any unjoined App Workspace ID.
4. **Migration:** Seed the existing default `OCTO_WORKSPACE_ID` as the initial `ri_console.workspace` row so zero existing operational rows or URLs break.
5. **UI Switcher:** Keep the UI at two levels (**Workspaces → Projects**) with role badges, live "New workspace…" (enabled for authenticated users, no `OCTO_ACCOUNT_KEY` gate), and "Invite teammate…" writing directly to `ri_console.workspace_member`.

## Eval / Completion Oracle
`POST /app-api/workspaces` creates a new App Workspace (`200`) and seeds owner membership without `OCTO_ACCOUNT_KEY`; `POST /app-api/workspaces/:id/invites` adds a pending `workspace_member` row that activates on first Google sign-in; a user cannot read or mutate an App Workspace or private Project they are not a member of (`403`); unit tests and live tenancy checks pass green.
