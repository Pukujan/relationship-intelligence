### Summary
Approval triage UX: Do/Don't, decline path, keyboard, optimistic. Approvals are cards with approve/decline but no explicit decline consequence, no keyboard triage, and no optimistic feedback. Borrowed patterns: Zapier human-approval Do/Don't with chosen decline path; Linear j/k triage.
**Research gate:** blocked by RI-0017 (#42); redesign requires research memo and owner sign-off first.


## PDD
Each proposal shows Do / Don't and states what a decline does (stop vs re-plan). j/k moves, a approves, d declines, e snoozes; counts and lists update optimistically and reconcile on server response; batch decide for low-risk groups.

## SDD
Decision route gains declinePath enum and idempotency key; client optimistic store with rollback on 409/502; keyboard handler scoped to the inbox view; telemetry span per decide with path.

## Eval / Completion Oracle
Parity: keyboard and button decides produce identical state; decline path honoured (stop leaves no mission, re-plan creates one proposal); double-decide 409; optimistic rollback visible on forced failure. Gates pass.
