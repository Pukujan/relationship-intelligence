### Summary
The RI-0023 slow crawler collected only headline/education/location. The owner asked for the rest of what a profile and session expose: connection lists (paginated, when the person's privacy setting shows them), recent posts with topic classification, company pages, follow lists, the home feed, email/contact info, and profile images displayed as clickable avatars in the app.

### PDD
One slow pass over a profile enriches the relationship graph with the person's actual network and activity: who they connect to, what they post and about what, which companies they are tied to, who they follow, what the owner's feed contains, how to contact them, and their photo shown in the UI. All of it stays slow, paced, sourced to the exact page URL, and never sends, follows, or posts anything back. Connections paginate only what LinkedIn actually renders for that profile — if privacy hides it, we record nothing rather than infer a network.

### SDD
1. Runner (collector/src/crawl.ts): after the profile header, gentle-scroll and paginate the Connections section (capped 300, deduped by URL); read the Contact info overlay when visible (email/phone/website/Twitter/GitHub); grab the top-card profile image URL; open the Posts/Activity tab, scroll gently and classify each post (product/hiring/fundraising/research/event/opinion/personal/other); collect company links from Experience; paginate the Following list (capped 200); then visit the home feed once and collect recent posts attributed separately.
2. Server (app/server/crawler.ts): Zod schema expanded for contact/imageUrl/connections/posts/companies/follows/feed. Contact fields and profile_image become sourced FACT claims with the profile URL as evidence. The five collection kinds are written as deduplicated ri_console.crawl_observation rows with a deterministic seedId and a matching FACT claim carrying the topic. On conflict, the row is skipped — replay writes nothing twice.
3. Display: WorldPerson and console Person carry avatarUrl; PersonAvatar renders the image when present (initials fallback otherwise); the PersonProfile header avatar links to the image; People list and CRM peek pass imageUrl too.
4. Failure boundary unchanged: a login wall/challenge/429 stops the run and cools the queue; no stealth, no retries around a block, no external writes back to LinkedIn.

### Eval / completion oracle
Metamorphic + holdout from RI-0023 still apply. New public tests (app/tests/crawlerExpansion.test.ts): contact FACTs, profile-image FACT + evidence URL, all five observation kinds persisted, post topics in ledger payload, replay writes no duplicates. Acceptance: runner and server typecheck; 140+ app tests green; CI gates green; production smoke shows a queue with connections/posts counts and a clickable avatar.
