### Summary
Imported event rows need domain-specific semantics; a roster is not proof of attendance and a series is not an individual edition.

## PDD
User filters an event roster, opens a participant or sponsor, and follows the evidence. In scope: event editions, participation roles, organization sponsors with role/tier, and sponsor history. No outreach or scoring model.

## SDD
Participation and sponsorship are typed claims with validity/evidence; attendance requires check-in or equivalent evidence; event series and editions are separate; current profile and roster are deterministic projections.

## Eval / Completion Oracle
Real event fixture integration; role filters return subsets; cross-edition leakage negative test; Playwright event→participant/sponsor→evidence; idempotent re-import.
