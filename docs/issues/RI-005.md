### Summary
The product needs agent research without direct, unreviewed model writes or hidden autonomous collection. This slice adds the durable queue and visible review workflow.

## PDD
User queues a person for research, sees status/cost/errors, reads proposed facts and source snapshots, and approves/rejects them. In scope: n8n/existing collectors, source caching, typed LLM extraction, human approval. No automated outreach or detection evasion.

## SDD
Research jobs/proposals are operational records; raw pages/files are stored first; proposals carry source, extraction/model version, and validation results; acceptance writes Octo claims/evidence with agent/source perspective. Octo Belief is not used as workflow approval.

## Eval / Completion Oracle
Real provider test in protected CI; unsourced proposal cannot write; approval idempotency; failure/resume path; Playwright queue→run→proposal→approve/reject; workspace isolation.
